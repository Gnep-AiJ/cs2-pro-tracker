# Player boards and public overlap

Scope: cs2pro only. Events remain first, FACEIT second. Use existing product/design skills and architecture; no new dependency. Rankings and two-player comparisons are the primary discovery actions. Short copy, semantic orange/cyan accents, genuine portraits, measured bars, foldable details and source evidence.

## Data

- Twenty explicitly joined professional identities in `app/data/pro-cohort.ts`, backed by the tracked immutable FACEIT IDs and saved HLTV entrant IDs. No nickname-only professional identity joins. Team labels are the maintained directory, not a promise of current rosters.
- Initial 2026-10-08 collection: 20/20 history and detailed-stat endpoints succeeded; 437 distinct finished matches, 22 observed player pairs. The stored snapshot records individual collection timestamps and actual sample dates. These counts describe this collection and will change.
- FACEIT requests: 30 recent public matches + 20 stat samples per player, maximum 40 upstream requests per scheduled run. Three concurrent player tasks. No profile fetch fanout when opening a leaderboard.
- Public same-side and opposing-side pairs use complete ten-player rosters and stable IDs. Deduplicate match IDs across histories; each pair is counted once per match. No party/friendship/training inference. Missing overlap does not imply no interaction.
- R2 bucket `cs2pro-data` stores immutable `observations/<timestamp>.json`, rolling `state/v1.json` and derived `public/v1.json`. Initial observations are preserved locally under ignored `outputs/pro-lab`. Public rolling history is at most 90 days / 5,000 matches; pagination or caps can omit matches. No claim of a complete history.
- Worker `cs2pro-data`, belonging exclusively to cs2pro, collects every six hours at minute 17 UTC. No public refresh/write endpoint. Secret contains only the FACEIT API key. A complete source outage preserves the last published data. Partial failure preserves prior stats with their original player timestamps.
- Event rankings use available provider samples, not a full professional leaderboard. ENSI /10 remains distinct from HLTV Rating and from FACEIT statistics. Future event metrics are withheld.

## Calculations

Take a player's newest 5/10/20 deduplicated stat samples first, then apply date, map and outcome filters. The default date period is 30 days before the collection timestamp; options are 7/30/90 days or any date. Undated samples are excluded when using a date period. ADR, K/R, HS% and kills are per-match means of present values; K/D is aggregate kills divided by aggregate deaths over complete kill/death observations (zero total deaths unavailable). Win rate excludes unknown outcomes. Rank eligibility uses at least 5/10 valid observations of the selected metric. Same values share a rank. Search retains cohort rank. Different players' actual dates/counts are visible.

The live arbitrary-account comparison remains available. It labels K/D as the per-match mean, whereas the curated data lab explicitly labels aggregate K/D in its method. No combined FACEIT/event skill score, win prediction or fabricated ELO history.
